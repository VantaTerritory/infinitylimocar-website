# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Executive assistants, corporate travel managers, event planners and private clients in Washington DC, Virginia and Maryland who need reliable chauffeured transportation — airport transfers, hourly service, groups — and want to request a quote quickly.

## Product Purpose

Infinity Limo Car (Springfield, VA, 13+ years) presents its car, limo and chauffeur service and turns visits into quote requests. Success: a visitor understands the service, the fleet and the coverage, and starts the quote in the hero without friction.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful · Precise · Personal. Warm, composed, quietly premium. Onyx, Gold and Ivory carry the brand; Oxblood and Stone are accents (never more than a third of a layout). Imagery: low, warm light, dark tones with one gold highlight.

## Anti-references

The generic limousine template: heavy gold gradients, stock photos of champagne, cluttered forms, walls of SEO text. Also avoid replicating the bugs of the old WordPress site (duplicate cards, empty pages, test pages in the sitemap).

## Design Principles

1. The quote is always one step away (hero widget, nav CTA, footer band).
2. Order and consistency earn trust: one type scale, one button, one panel system.
3. Real facts over adjectives: airports, coverage, fleet capacities, memberships.
4. Mobile first for the quote flow.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible focus, labeled fields, native disclosures, reduced-motion support.
